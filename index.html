The Founder

Desiree — Artist, Educator, Visionary

My love for makeup began with a fascination for the perfect smoky eye — and that curiosity became a career. My first role was at MAC Cosmetics, where I spent years mastering the craft and falling deeply in love with the artistry of beauty.

After MAC, I launched Spark Beauty Co. and never looked back. I've trained at the Goar Avetisyan School of Beauty in Russia and the Samer Khouzami Academy in Beirut, Lebanon — and my international education journey is far from over.

Today, I lead a team serving brides, editorial clients, events, and aspiring artists across Arizona. My mission is to welcome every ethnicity, celebrate every skin tone, and ignite a spark of confidence in everyone who sits in my chair.

Before You Book

Non-Bridal Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

This policy applies to all non-bridal appointments and services booked with Spark Beauty Co.

Retainer & Appointment Reservation

A 50% non-refundable retainer is required at the time of booking. Your appointment is not reserved until the retainer has been received. The retainer is applied toward your scheduled service but is non-refundable under all circumstances.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

If an appointment is cancelled or the client is unable to attend, the 50% retainer is forfeited. A new 50% retainer is required to book another appointment.

In the event of a verified emergency or exceptional life circumstance, Spark Beauty Co., at its sole discretion, may allow the amount already paid to be transferred as a one-time service credit. The rescheduled appointment must take place within 12 months of the original appointment date and is subject to availability. After 12 months, any unused credit expires.

Any difference in pricing between the original appointment and the rescheduled service will be the client’s responsibility. Service credits have no cash value and cannot be refunded or transferred to another person.

No-Shows

Failure to attend a scheduled appointment without notice is considered a no-show. No-shows are responsible for 100% of the scheduled service total. The remaining balance may be charged to the payment method on file. No-show payments are non-refundable and are not eligible for a future service credit.

Late Arrivals

Clients arriving late may have their service time shortened to avoid impacting subsequent appointments. If the service cannot reasonably be completed due to the client’s late arrival, the appointment may be treated as a same-day cancellation and all amounts paid will be forfeited.

Policy Acknowledgement

By booking an appointment and submitting your retainer, you acknowledge and agree to Spark Beauty Co.’s Non-Bridal Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy.
